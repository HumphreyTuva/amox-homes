About Us

A division of AMOX Solutions Limited.

AMOXHomes is a division of AMOX Solutions Limited, established to address challenges surrounding housing and settlement. Our initial focus is on students and professionals, particularly individuals coming to Malindi for university, college, attachment, internship, employment, business or other opportunities.

AMOXHomes works with landlords, property owners, internet providers, household item providers, moving services and other relevant businesses, creating an interconnected housing ecosystem.

Vision

To become Africa's trusted platform for housing connections and settlement support for students and professionals relocating to new places.

Mission

To make finding a home and settling into a new place simple and convenient.
